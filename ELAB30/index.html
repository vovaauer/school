3 Reasons You Should Never Install an Ad Blocker

By Albert Auer

Are you thinking of installing an ad blocker? Maybe you saw someone using one, or you just got annoyed by all the ads. Don't, because if you do you'd be making a huge mistake, and here's why.

It's way too much effort

Installing an ad blocker requires an immense amount of effort. You have to:

  • Look up uBlock Origin
  • Click on the web store result
  • Add the extension

That's like 3 whole things, and for what? Just to never see another ad again and save hours of your life? Yeah, sounds way too difficult.

The ads know you so well

How else are you going to know what you want? The advertisers know everything about you. For example, they know that you need to buy new shoes because they know you bought some 2 weeks ago. Or how they know you want to know the super secret way to make millions of dollars.

And think of the poor creators

Lastly, the money. Every ad you watch sends money straight to the creator behind whatever you're looking at. Well, after the platform takes their cut. And then the ad network. And then the data partners. And then the ad exchange. And then the agency. And then, the rest goes to the creator, and you can feel good knowing the $0.0001 the content ends up getting is going to really help save them from going bankrupt.
